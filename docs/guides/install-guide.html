10 of 16Using it safely

Control this Mac

A trial

It lets a bot look at your screen, click and type. What it needs: docs/CONNECTORS.md

The first action of a reply waits on a card. On it, Allow for this turn lets the rest of that reply go on without asking again.

While any card is open, no screen action runs, so a bot cannot press Approve for you.

After 64 rounds in one reply, the bot asks on a card to keep going. Deny ends the reply and keeps what it wrote.

11 of 16Using it safely

macOS will ask

System Settings → Privacy & Security

Asked only when a feature first needs it: Automation for Mail, Messages, Contacts, Notes and Chrome; Calendars for Calendar; Contacts for names in the Messages chat picker; Accessibility and Screen Recording for Control this Mac.

Full Disk Access, for your Messages history, is never asked. Add OpenBots Next yourself, then quit and reopen it.

You can refuse or withdraw any of them.

After a rebuild, macOS may ask again. If it asks whether the Google helper may use your Keychain, allow it.

12 of 16If something goes wrong

In Terminal

This installer is for macOS.
This build targets Apple Silicon (arm64).

It needs a Mac with Apple Silicon.

This build needs full Xcode 16.3+ (Swift 6.1 toolchain) at /Applications/Xcode.app.

Get Xcode, open it once, run the line again.

The build failed. Nothing was installed.

Check Xcode, then run the line again.

Claude's noteThe installer checks only that Xcode is there, not its version or its first open.

OPENBOTS_GOOGLE_OAUTH_CLIENT_ID must look like 1234-abc.apps.googleusercontent.com (the Client ID, not the secret).

Put in the Client ID, not the secret.
